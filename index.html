Services

Web design, development, and local SEO for small businesses

Most projects use more than one of these. Here's what each one is, who it's for, and what you walk away with.

01

Custom Web Design

A site designed from scratch around your business. No template with your logo dropped in. We plan the pages, shape the message, and design every screen for the way your customers actually look for a company like yours.

Who it's for
Service businesses with no website, or one that no longer looks like the company they've become.
The problem it solves
People size you up online before they call, and what they find doesn't match the quality of your work.
What you get
A custom website design, phone layouts for every page, and the source files. You own all of it.

02

Website Development

The build itself. Clean, fast code, forms that actually reach your inbox, and tap-to-call wherever someone might want it.

Who it's for
Businesses that have a design or a clear plan and need it built properly.
The problem it solves
A slow or fragile site that breaks on phones or quietly loses leads.
What you get
A tested, launched website running on your own domain and hosting.

03

Website Redesign

We keep what's working, fix what isn't, and rebuild the rest. That starts with looking at where visitors lose interest on the site you have now.

Who it's for
Businesses with a site that's a few years old and no longer pulling its weight.
The problem it solves
Dated design, confusing navigation, and pages that never ask anyone to do anything.
What you get
A restructured, redesigned site, with your old pages pointed to the new ones so existing search traffic isn't thrown away.
Start a project →
04

Local SEO

Local SEO services for businesses that depend on nearby customers. We set up or clean up your Google Business Profile, tighten your service and location pages, and make sure your details match everywhere they appear. Nobody can promise a ranking. What we can do is give Google every reason to show you.

Who it's for
Small businesses whose customers search by city, by neighborhood, or with "near me."
The problem it solves
Competitors show up in the map results and you don't.
What you get
A Google Business Profile setup or cleanup, on-page SEO for your key pages, and a plain-English list of what to keep doing.
Start a project →
05

Website Strategy

A working session and a written plan for owners who aren't sure what they need yet. We look at your customers, your competitors, and your current site, then map out what to build and in what order.

Who it's for
Owners who want a clear plan before they spend money on a build.
The problem it solves
Guessing at what the website should say or do.
What you get
A page-by-page plan, messaging notes, and priorities you can hand to us or to anyone else.

06

Landing Pages

One focused page for one service, one offer, or one ad campaign. One message and one action.

Who it's for
Businesses running ads, promoting something seasonal, or launching a new service.
The problem it solves
Paying for clicks that land on a homepage trying to say everything at once.
What you get
A designed and built landing page with a working lead form and tracking set up.

Akira Digital is a web design and local SEO company for small and growing service businesses. It's founder-led, so the person you talk to on the first call is the person leading your project.

Questions

What people ask before they start

How much does a small business website cost?

Our packages start at $1,000 for a three-page site and go up with the number of pages and how much strategy and SEO work is involved. Every package is listed with its price in the pricing section, so you'll know the number before we ever talk.

How long does it take to build a business website?

It depends on the size of the site and how quickly content and feedback come together. You'll get a realistic timeline in writing before anything starts.

Do you only work with businesses in Los Angeles?

No. We're based in Los Angeles and work remotely with service businesses anywhere in the United States.

Does a small business need local SEO?

If your customers search for what you do by city, by neighborhood, or with "near me," then yes. Local SEO is what helps you show up in map results and local listings when they do.

Will I own my website?

Yes. Your domain, your hosting account, and your files are yours. If you ever move on, all of it goes with you.

Are the website concepts real client sites?

No. They're original design concepts built around fictional businesses. We made them to show how we'd approach different industries.

What do I need to get started?

A short conversation. Tell us about the business and what you want the site to do, and we'll take it from there.
